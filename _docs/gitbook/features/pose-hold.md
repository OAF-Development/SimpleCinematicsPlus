# Pose Hold

Full-body emotes normally drop back to idle when the game thinks you've moved or interacted. Pose Hold stops that while the camera is open, so a pose stays on screen for the whole shot.

* Tick **Hold Pose ( , )** in the Sequencer section, or press `,` (comma).
* It works with vanilla emotes and with **SurvivorAnimations** poses.
* It only applies while the cinematic camera is open. Close the camera and emotes behave normally again.

> [!TIP]
SurvivorAnimations isn't required; but you will reap it's benefits if chosing to do so.
